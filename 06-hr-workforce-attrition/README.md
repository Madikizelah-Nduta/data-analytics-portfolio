# HR Workforce & Attrition
**Sector:** People analytics  |  **Tool:** Excel  |  **Data:** synthetic

> All data in this project is synthetic and generated for demonstration. Fee rates, targets and thresholds are illustrative.

## Business question
Which departments and tenure bands lose people, why do they leave, and what does turnover cost?

## Data
300 synthetic employee records across 6 departments.

## What I built
- 12-month attrition rate against a 15% target
- Regrettable-loss flag (leavers rated 4 or above)
- Tenure bands, exit reasons and replacement-cost model

## Key results (from the workbook)
- Annual attrition 25.6% vs 15% target (62 leavers, 23 regrettable)
- Estimated replacement cost of KES 34.4M
- IT lost 52.6% of its team and Sales 29.4%
- Pay and career growth account for about 55% of exits

## Skills shown
Excel (AVERAGEIFS, SUMIFS, IFERROR, date logic), people analytics, cost modelling
