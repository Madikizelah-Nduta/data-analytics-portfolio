# Loan Portfolio & Credit Risk
**Sector:** Finance  |  **Tool:** Excel  |  **Data:** synthetic

> All data in this project is synthetic and generated for demonstration. Fee rates, targets and thresholds are illustrative.

## Business question
How healthy is the loan book, where is the risk concentrated, and are provisions adequate?

## Data
400 synthetic loans across 6 branches and 5 products.

## What I built
- PAR30 and PAR90 measures against a 5% target
- Ageing buckets with a provisioning estimate
- Branch and product risk tables with automatic flags

## Key results (from the workbook)
- KES 79.4M gross loan book; PAR30 13.3% vs 5% target; PAR90 4.6%
- Only 1 of 6 branches (Nakuru) is within target; Sotik PAR30 is 23.8%
- Agribusiness loans have the highest 90-day risk (8.2%)
- Provisions of KES 5.36M cover about 146% of the PAR90 balance

## Skills shown
Excel (nested IF, INDEX/MATCH, SUMIFS, COUNTIFS), credit risk, provisioning
