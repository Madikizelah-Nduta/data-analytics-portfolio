# Logistics & Supplier Delivery
**Sector:** Operations  |  **Tool:** Excel  |  **Data:** synthetic

> All data in this project is synthetic and generated for demonstration. Fee rates, targets and thresholds are illustrative.

## Business question
Which suppliers deliver on time and in full, what do late and damaged deliveries cost, and is performance improving?

## Data
500 synthetic deliveries from 6 suppliers to 6 regions.

## What I built
- OTIF (on time, in full) flags and supplier grades
- Late-delivery penalty cost and damage rate
- Region table and monthly trend

## Key results (from the workbook)
- OTIF 75.0% vs a 90% target; on-time 85.4%
- Lakeside Hardware has 44.8% OTIF and causes KES 141,000 of KES 337,500 penalties
- Three suppliers grade C
- Bomet has the weakest in-full rate (77.2%)

## Skills shown
Excel (IF logic, SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, INDEX/MATCH), supplier scorecards, operations analytics
